# Offer stack: ladder and value stacks

Draft 2026-11-21. Prices in [brackets] are proposals. Lock them before anything goes live.

The model: the one-time build pays for acquisition, and recurring revenue is the business. Every sale leads to a next one, and every build feeds recurring through seats that expire at 90 days.

Content rule stays in force (brand guide v11): no tier names or framework names in reels. Rep reels point to Rep Room. Coach reels point to the scorecard.

## 0. Scorecard (free)

Job: qualify the coach and book the call. Nothing else.

- Questions: offer price, monthly revenue, who takes the calls, lead response time, show rate, close rate, follow-up after a no or a no-show, CRM in use.
- Output: a scored readout showing which parts of the sales system are missing.
- Routing in GHL:
  - Offer $2K+ and revenue $20K+/mo → calendar for a sales call.
  - Below that → Rep Room or the monthly live training.
- Also the ticket into the monthly live training (see Warm room below).

## Mechanics

**Price lock.** Membership rates stay locked while the subscription stays active. Cancel and rejoin at the current price.

**Deposit (fair version).** [$1,000] holds a build cohort slot.
- Book the build within 30 days and the full deposit comes off the price.
- Don't book it within 30 days and the full [$1,000] becomes Closer Room credit, worth [3] months of one seat. The buyer keeps the full value.
- Full Team Install deposits are refundable on request until the proposal is signed.

**Seats that expire.** Every build includes 90 days of Closer Room seats. See `02-ghl-seat-expiration.md`.

**Warm room.** One live training a month for coaches, 60 minutes on one part of the sales system. The scorecard is the ticket in, and booking calls happen live. Offer the next build cohort at the end. Same job as their live event, run every month.

**Upgrade paths.**
- Rep Room → Closer Room: 2 live calls in a row, or 3 posted wins.
- Build → Managed: day 60 report shows gaps the team isn't closing on its own.
- Managed → Full Team Install: coach crosses $75K/mo, or hires a second closer.
