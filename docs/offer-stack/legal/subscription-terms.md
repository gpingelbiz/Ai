## Automatic renewal

**Your membership renews automatically at the end of each billing period and is charged to your payment method on file until you cancel.**

- After checkout you get a confirmation email with the plan, price, billing frequency, renewal date and how to cancel.
- For any annual plan, we email a reminder 15 to 45 days before each renewal.
- If a renewal payment fails, we may retry it and pause access until it succeeds.

## Price lock

Your rate stays the same for as long as your subscription stays active. If you cancel and rejoin later, the current price applies. For any plan without a price lock, we email you at least 30 days before a price change, and you can cancel before it applies.

## Seats included with a build

Closer Room seats included with a Sales System Build or Full Team Install are free for the period in your Scope (usually 90 days) and **do not renew automatically**. They end on the date in your Scope unless you choose a paid plan. We'll remind you before they end.

## Free trials

If an offer includes a free trial, the trial length and end date are shown at signup. You won't be charged during the trial. If the trial asks for a payment method, it converts to the paid plan at the price shown unless you cancel before the trial ends, and we'll email you a reminder 3 to 21 days before it ends. One trial per person.
