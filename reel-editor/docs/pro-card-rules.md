# Pro look card rules (Greg 2026-11-19: "make new rules based off all the cards we just built and the deep research")

Sources: ~/Reels/research/2026-11-19-pro-reels (9 lanes) and the 50 approved card designs (Desktop/Reels/2026-11-18 Clips from long video/New look stills).

1. Earned by the words. A card only appears when Greg's words fit its family (below). Spoken slots are his exact words, revealed when he says them. Labels are at most 4 words and never contain a number; every number on a card is one he says. (Research: irrelevant detail lowers recall; Greg: no made-up numbers.)
2. Families. distinction (compare, correction, do/don't, myth/truth, before/after, thumbs, poll, signpost, venn; since 11-20 also versus split, scale balance, toggle switch, spectrum, you say/they hear, true/false, verdict stamp, rookie/pro, word swap, either/or), procedure (steps, checklist, timeline, formula, countdown, ranking; also stack fan, roadmap, branch, funnel, cycle, staircase, pill tracker, first/then/finally, pyramid, recipe), proof (stat, percent ring, bars, rating, progress ring, stopwatch, loading bar; also odometer, scoreboard, line chart, gauge, money stack, trophy, streak, case study, KPI tile, testimonial), conversation (objection, text thread, incoming call, email, notification, ask this, script highlighter, search bar; also DM chat, voicemail, video call, comment, CRM note, dialer, typing, quick replies, call transcript, lock screen), warning (red flag, cost, receipt, pros/cons; also stop sign, caution tape, crossed out, trap), payoff (rule, tip badge, quote, takeaway banner, gold frame, big word, sticky note, green flag, unlock, calendar; also marker highlight, newspaper, seal, key insight, lightbulb, save this), emphasis (underline, arrow, label). A spoken slot may carry its own check (`validate(text, slots)`): a proof card's number must be one he says, a streak counts to 7.
3. Rhythm. About one card per 6 to 8 s of talk: at most 5 card starts in any 30 s, at least 2.5 s between starts, and never two cards on screen at once (an underline on a caption word does not count). On-footage cards may cover up to 70% of any 30 s; Greg stays visible behind them.
4. Variety. No card type twice in a reel (in reels over 45 s, not within 20 s), no family twice in a row, no two light (bone) cards in a row. (Research: repeating one composition regardless of what is said is what looks templated.)
5. Full screen. At most one full-screen card a reel, only for a list or something meant to be read, at most 4.5 s on screen.
6. Clean edges. Nothing before the hook ends (2.4 s) and nothing starting in the last 3 s, so the ending holds still.
7. Placement. Never over Greg's face (head + 56 px), the mic (+24 px) or the caption band. Upper area first, then torso on an opaque card.
8. Motion and sound. Enter 233 ms cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) with at most 16 px travel, state changes 300 ms, exit 167 ms, write-in reveals on the spoken word, held 2 s after the last reveal. One soft tick per card at most; labels and underlines are silent.
9. Word marks. Underline only, drawn under the word. No circles: they covered the words (Greg 2026-11-19).
10. Designs and tags (2026-11-27, the Herk Edit Kit import). A type may have several designs: a design sets `variantOf: "<type>"` and takes exactly that type's slots. The planner sees one line per type (with its tags) and the editor picks the design from the words, so a reel always gets the same design and different reels get different ones; "one type per reel" counts every design of it. Every card carries 1-3 tags from `TAG_WORDS` (number, list, comparison, quote and so on) so the element mix can pick by kind.
11. Safe zone. Every card sits inside the shared safe zone (`src/safe-zone.mjs`): 150 px off both sides, clear of the right button column from 35% of the height down, under the top bar and above the bottom strip at 1600 px. On-footage cards are at most 720 px wide; a full-screen card keeps its words inside x 180-900 and y 200-1600.
12. Herk Edit Kit designs (`src/pro-cards/herk-*.mjs`) are restyled from the kit's MIT cards (copyright 2026 Nate Herk, `src/pro-cards/HERK-LICENSE.md`); `herk-triage-*.json` records what happened to each of its 406 cards.
