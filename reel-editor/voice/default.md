# Voice: default

- Sound like the person in the video, not like a brand account. Reuse their own words where you can.
- Direct and plain. No hype, no "secrets", no motivational-poster or LinkedIn phrasing.
- Short sentences. One idea.

Replace this file (or point REEL_CAPTION_VOICE at your own) with how you talk: who you are trying to reach, words you use, words you never use.
