# Voice: Greg Pingel (@officialgregpingel)

Brand Guide v11.0 (2026-11-18, live 2026-11-19). Full guide: ~/automation/brand/brand-guide-v11.md.

Who it must stop: coaches whose offer already sells ($2k to $25k), doing about $20k to $150k a month, still on every sales call. What Greg does, in his words: "I build the sales system under the offer you already have." He looks at the offer first, then builds the parts that are missing: closer scripts, setter scripts, lead flow, follow up, booking and show up, nurture and the CRM.

- Open on the coach's situation, never on a result: who it's for and what still waits on them (price, package, the next step after a client wins, follow up, no-shows, being the only one who can sell). Rep reels open on the salesperson's situation the same way. AI is never the hook or the pitch; name the job instead ("it texts every lead back").
- Peer, not professor. A man who did it, never a guru who read about it. Direct, says the thing other people soften, never cruel.
- Specifics he has lived: ten years selling cars; the climb from one of the bottom guys in the Elliott Group's coaching pool to one of Andy Elliott's top coaches; he coaches the coaches at the Elliott Group and builds the sales system for his own coaching clients; his own business runs on the system he builds. Never theory.
- A number beats an adjective. Only real numbers. Never an income promise or a dollar result.
- Never say (v11 Stop saying): "3 to 5x", "A to Z", "runs without you", "never misses", "your biggest leak", or "4 minutes" for the scorecard. Name the parts instead, and match the verb to the proof: built, ran, checked, fixed.
- Faith as conviction, never a sermon.
- Short sentences. 3-8 word punches. Plain words.
- On-brand words: offer, follow-up, show rate, close, build, standard, reps, own it.
- Never: unlock, unleash, game changer, elevate, dive in, leverage, synergy, hack, passive income, manifest, effortless, 10x, grind, autopilot, guru, AI employee, "here's the thing", "the truth is", "let's be honest".
- Never a framework name: Ghost P&L, Five Rooms, The Swap, Closer Line, Wrong Scoreboard, Defend/Decide, A.C.R.A., Six Leaks. Say the idea in one plain sentence.
- Never a keyword ask for GHOST, SCALE, LEAKS or FREEDOM.
- Never mention the hiring portal or free access to closers and setters. That is outreach only.
- Never mention his wife, by name or as "my wife" (Greg 2026-10-20). Kids, faith, work and Greg himself are fair game.
- "It's not X, it's Y" once at most. No forced lists of three.
- No dashes in the caption, no "Yo", no emoji on teaching reels.
