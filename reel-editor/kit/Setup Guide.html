Reel Editor: Setup Guide

A walkthrough for your first setup. Allow about 45 minutes, including signing in. Keep this page open while you work. Press Return when the installer asks you to continue.

Step 0: get the kit onto your Mac

  1. If the kit is on your phone, use Share, then AirDrop, and choose your Mac. Accept it on the Mac. Or open the email, Google Drive link or Dropbox link on your Mac and download the zip there.
  2. If the kit came in a text and the file shows up blank or will not open, ask Greg for a download link instead. Text messages often drop zip files.
  3. In Finder, open Downloads (or wherever you saved it). Double click the zip to unzip it. Open the new kit folder.
  4. Open Setup Guide.html first. Keep the whole kit together, including its Brand folder. You will open the installer in Part 4.

Part 1. What this is

Reel Editor runs on your Mac. You film yourself talking and drop the video into a folder. It cuts pauses, adds word captions and a hook title, and can add B-roll (short clips showing what you mean), graphic cards and music. Some features need the optional accounts below. You get a finished video and a written caption to review.

Your reels and posts include scheduling through your own Metricool account. Film, drop your clip in a Raw folder in Desktop/Reels, watch the finished reel, then drag it into Desktop/Reels/Publish. The Claude app schedules approved reels within the hour during posting hours. Carousels and statics are made and scheduled by the Claude app at 6 AM and noon. Keep your Mac on and the Claude app open. Once you fill in your brand guide, it can also make photo posts, carousels, optional story games and a weekly filming plan.

At the end of setup, the Claude app opens and a setup prompt is copied. Open the Code tab, paste, then press Return. Claude sets up reel-publish and daily-posts. Answer yes to its questions, and when it says the routines are on you are done. Carousels and statics are made and scheduled by the Claude app at 6 AM and noon. Keep your Mac on and the Claude app open.

Part 2. How it works

  1. Film a talking clip on your phone. About 25 to 45 seconds is a useful starting point.
  2. AirDrop it to your Mac, then move it into a Raw folder inside Desktop/Reels.
  3. The Mac edits in the background and notifies you when it finishes. Timing depends on the clip, your Mac and the services used.
  4. Open the finished reel in Desktop/Reels and watch it before sharing it.

After watching a finished reel, drag its folder into Desktop/Reels/Publish. That is your approval to post. The Claude app schedules it within the hour during posting hours. Carousels and statics are made and scheduled by the Claude app at 6 AM and noon. Keep your Mac on and the Claude app open. Made holds copies and scheduling notes. STATUS.txt reports things that need attention. Metricool sends the post to Instagram and any other networks you chose during setup.

The files and settings live on your Mac. Connected AI services process material for the features you use. Use your own accounts. Greg does not receive your files or health alerts automatically.

Part 3. What you need before you start

Use a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), about 4 GB of free space and internet. Open the Apple menu, then About This Mac. Look for a Chip name beginning with Apple M. In System Settings, General, Storage, check your available space.

Have your Claude Pro or Max account ready for the brand guide walkthrough and writing features. Optional services unlock the features shown below. The kit's Sign up here folder has links you can double click.

Connect Metricool to Claude before installing

  1. Create your Metricool account and connect your Instagram inside Metricool.
  2. Sign in at claude.ai. Open Settings, then Connectors.
  3. Find Metricool, click Connect, and sign in with your own Metricool account.

Leave your Mac on and connected for scheduled work. A sleeping Mac runs missed calendar jobs after waking; a shut down Mac cannot edit or post. Check the status after turning it back on.

  3. A Terminal window opens. It shows progress as it installs the tools. If Apple asks to install command line tools, click Install, wait until it finishes, then open the kit installer again. A password typed into Terminal may show no letters; type it and press Return.

Part 6. Fill in your brand guide with Claude Code

You build your own brand. Do not ask Claude to invent your history, proof or results. Open Terminal in Applications, Utilities. Type claude and press Return. Sign in if asked. Share your website address, bio, notes and captions you like.

Paste this exact request:

Help me fill in my brand guide at Desktop/Reels/Brand/brand-guide.md, one section at a time, and ask me questions.

Work through who you are, your audience, offer, story, beliefs, topics, voice, visual style, proof and call to action. Check every answer. Delete the line NOT FILLED IN YET only when the guide is actually complete. Extra features that require a finished guide stay on hold until then.

Paste this request:

Help me create my own brand guide at ~/.reel/brand-guide.md, one section at a time, and ask me questions. Use only facts I give you. Help me update ~/.reel/voice.md from my answers, with a backup first.

Work through your audience, offer, story, topics, voice, colors, fonts and real proof. Read each section before accepting it.

Then paste this exact request to set your reel look:

Set my reel colors and fonts in ~/.reel/my-brand.json from my brand guide. Back up the file first, change only my own files, then run reel brand ~/.reel/my-brand.json --check.

If Claude reports validation problems, ask it to fix those before editing another clip. Keep the backup so you can restore your previous look.

Part 7. Your daily routine

  1. Film with the phone at eye level and window light in front of you. Start talking promptly. If you stumble, pause and repeat the sentence; review the edit afterward.
  2. AirDrop to your Mac. For a preview, move the clip into Desktop/Reels/Raw - Reel with B-roll.
  3. Wait for the notification. Open the finished reel in Desktop/Reels, check the words and watch it before sharing.

For scheduling, watch the finished reel and drag it into Desktop/Reels/Publish. Carousels and statics are made and scheduled by the Claude app at 6 AM and noon. Keep your Mac on and the Claude app open. Check Made and Metricool afterward. To publish an edit you reviewed, drag its finished folder into Desktop/Reels/Publish.

For personal B-roll, film short 6 to 10 second shots of your workspace, a walk or everyday activities, without faces. Give each file a descriptive name such as coffee-pouring.mov and put it in Raw - My B-roll clips.

Part 8. What happens on its own

The drop folder watches for new clips. Personalized client kits also check for software fixes weekly and waiting reels three times a day. Updates preserve your settings and brand; local code edits pause updates. A failed update check rolls back the software. Plain Reel Editor does not post for you.

  • Reels are scheduled on the next day without a reel at the best available hour, keeping a two hour gap from other posts.
  • New studios use reels.trial: "both": a normal reel plus a separate Instagram Trial Reel. Existing settings are kept. The Trial copy may need a later slot.
  • Health checks run three times a day. Notifications and STATUS.txt report problems.
  • Once your guide is complete, a Sunday filming pack provides six ideas and three B-roll ideas for the coming week.
  • Story games are optional and off initially. Ask Claude to set stories.enabled to true in studio.json after completing your guide.
  • Daily carousels and graphic posts use configured time slots. Run the installer again to set times when you are ready. With both slots set to none, those daily photo posts stay off.

Part 9. Fix a problem

What you seeWhat to do
Nothing happens after a dropUse one of the Raw folders, not the main Reels folder. Keep the Mac on. If you see a Reel failed notification, read it, then rename the clip to retry.
A caption word is wrongRead check these words.txt in the reel folder. Ask Claude Code to add the correction to ~/.reel/dictionary.json, with a backup first.
The hook covers your faceAdd #top or #low to the clip name and drop it again.
A post did not go outRead Desktop/Reels/STATUS.txt and Not posted.txt in its Made folder. If Claude signed out, open Terminal, type claude and sign in. If Metricool disconnected, reconnect at claude.ai, Settings, Connectors.
macOS blocks the installerUse System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway as described in Part 4.
Anything elseOpen Install Reel Editor.command again to update or repair. Existing settings are kept, but read any questions before accepting defaults. If you are still stuck, text Greg with the message you see.
