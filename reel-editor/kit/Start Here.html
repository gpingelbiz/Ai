Reel Editor

Drop a raw talking-head clip in a folder and get back a finished vertical reel: captions, tight cuts, a hook title placed around your face, a check on every word, and a ready-to-post caption.

You need: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), about 4 GB of free space, and internet. Optional: an ElevenLabs account for the most accurate transcription.

1. Install (about 10 minutes)

  1. Double-click Install Reel Editor.command in this folder. A Terminal window opens and does the work.
  2. Answer four questions:
    QuestionWhat to pick
    Your look2 for plain white captions, or 3 for your own colors (two hex codes, like #00AAFF). Pick 1 only if you make content for Greg Pingel.
    ElevenLabs keyPaste it, or press Return to skip. Get one at elevenlabs.io, under Developers, API Keys.
    Drop folderPress Return for yes.
    CodexPress Return for yes if you have a ChatGPT account, then sign in in the browser window. It adds b-roll and a carousel to every reel.
  3. It finishes by playing a short test reel. That means it worked.

If your Mac says the file "can't be opened" or "Apple could not verify" it:

  1. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll down to the message about Install Reel Editor.command and click Open Anyway.
  3. Confirm, and enter your Mac password if asked.

Or skip the file: open Terminal (in Applications, Utilities), paste this line and press Return:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gpingelbiz/Ai/main/reel-editor/install.sh | bash

If a window asks to install Apple's command line tools, click Install, wait for it to finish, then run the installer again. If Homebrew is missing, the installer adds it and asks for your Mac password once.

2. Make a reel

  1. Drop a clip into one of the three Raw folders in Desktop/Reels. The folder decides what you get.
  2. Wait for the notification. A 1-minute clip takes about 3 minutes.
  3. Open the new folder in Desktop/Reels, named by date and number.

For background music, put licensed tracks (mp3, m4a or wav, such as Epidemic Sound downloads) in Desktop/Reels/Music: the editor picks one that fits what you say (name files Mood - Title - Artist.mp3), low under your voice, and music.txt names it. Every folder also gets:

B-roll and carousels use Codex: say yes when the installer offers it, and sign in with your ChatGPT account. Without it, Raw - Reel still works. Codex has usage limits; when it hits one, b-roll reels come without b-roll for 20 minutes and a carousel leaves a note to drop it again.

3. Make it yours

  • Your words: open ~/.reel/dictionary.json and add your program and framework names under terms. They get highlighted and spelled right. People and company names go under names: spelled right, not highlighted.
  • Your caption voice: edit ~/.reel/voice.md with who you talk to, words you use, and words you never use.
  • A call-to-action: add export REEL_WATCH_CTA="Comment YOURWORD" inside the reel-editor block in ~/.zshrc.
  • Change your look: double-click the installer again and pick another option.

Content Studio: your posts, made and scheduled for you

Your kit also sets up the Content Studio: one folder that makes your carousels, statics and reels and schedules them into your own Metricool, every day, by itself. Nobody has to check the carousels or statics. All you do is keep dropping photos and clips.

You need, on top of the above: your own Claude account (Pro or Max), your own Metricool account with your Instagram connected, and the Mac switched on. The daily run goes at about 6:07; if the Mac is asleep then, it runs when the Mac wakes.

Install

  1. Double-click Install Content Studio.command (not the Reel Editor one). It installs the reel editor in your brand first, then asks six things:
    QuestionWhat to do
    Your photosDrag the folder with your photos into the Terminal window and press Return, or press Return to use Content Studio/Photos. A folder on the Desktop, in Documents or in Downloads can't be read by a background job on a Mac, so the installer offers to copy those photos in. Photos from the Photos app: select them, File, Export, into this folder.
    ClaudeIf Claude isn't signed in, it opens here: sign in with your Claude account, then type /exit and press Return. Claude writes your posts in your voice and talks to Metricool for you.
    MetricoolOn claude.ai open Settings, Connectors, find Metricool, click Connect and sign in with your Metricool account (the installer opens the page). Then pick your brand from the list.
    How images reach MetricoolSee the three ways below. Press Return for the first one if you're not sure.
    Posting timesReturn keeps a carousel at 10:00 and a static at 18:00 every day in your time zone, or type your own.
    First postReturn makes one post right away so you can see it work (it lands in Made and in Metricool).

How your images reach Metricool (you pick one)

Metricool needs a link to each image or video for a moment, then keeps its own copy. Your kit can give it that link three ways:

WayHow it worksYou need
1. Straight from this Mac (recommended)While a post is being scheduled, the Mac shares only that post's files at a private, random address for about a minute. Metricool copies them and the address closes. Nothing is left online except your post in Metricool.Nothing extra.

The Content Studio itself runs on a Mac (it uses the Mac's face finder and background jobs). Without a Mac, the posts can't be made; the image route only decides where the finished files go.

In Content StudioWhat it is
Photos(or the folder you picked) Where posts take photos from. Each photo is used once and never again. Blurry, dark, washed-out, tiny shots, screenshots and photos with a cut-off face are skipped by themselves.

studio.jsonYour settings: photo folder, posting times, Metricool brand, image route, AI label. Edit it in TextEdit and save; the next run uses it.

What happens every morning

  1. At about 6:07 the studio looks at the next three days and finds every posting time that has no post yet (one you scheduled yourself in Metricool counts).
  2. For each, it picks the job by your brand guide's pillar mix, writes the post in your voice from your guide, picks photos you haven't used (matching each photo to its slide), and sets the slides in your colors and fonts.
  3. It puts a copy in Made and schedules it in your Metricool. A post that breaks one of your rules (a word from your never-say list) goes in as a draft with the reason instead of posting.
  4. When something fails you get a Mac notification; the post waits in Made with Not posted.txt, and the next run tries again.

AI label: any post with a picture made by AI carries Instagram's "made with AI" label, always. Your real photos are never labelled. Set "ai": {"label": "always"} in studio.json to label every post.

Pictures made by AI (for slides with no photo left) need Codex, and hooks that follow what is winning need the Sandcastles connector in Claude. Both are optional: without them the studio uses only your photos and your guide.

What you seeFix

Remove it

In Terminal, run ~/Ai/reel-editor/scripts/install-watcher.sh --uninstall to turn off the drop folder. Then delete the folders ~/Ai, ~/.reel and ~/.reel-venv, and the lines between # >>> reel-editor >>> and # <<< reel-editor <<< in ~/.zshrc. Your reels in Desktop/Reels stay.
