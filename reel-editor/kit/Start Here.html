Reel Editor

Drop a raw talking-head clip in a folder and get back a finished vertical reel: captions, tight cuts, a hook title placed around your face, a check on every word, and a ready-to-post caption.

You need: a Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer), about 4 GB of free space, and internet. Optional: an ElevenLabs account for the most accurate transcription.

1. Install (about 10 minutes)

  1. Double-click Install Reel Editor.command in this folder. A Terminal window opens and does the work.
  2. Answer four questions:
    QuestionWhat to pick
    Your look2 for plain white captions, or 3 for your own colors (two hex codes, like #00AAFF). Pick 1 only if you make content for Greg Pingel.
    ElevenLabs keyPaste it, or press Return to skip. Get one at elevenlabs.io, under Developers, API Keys.
    Drop folderPress Return for yes.
    CodexPress Return for yes if you have a ChatGPT account, then sign in in the browser window. It adds b-roll and a carousel to every reel.
  3. It finishes by playing a short test reel. That means it worked.

If your Mac says the file "can't be opened" or "Apple could not verify" it:

  1. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
  2. Scroll down to the message about Install Reel Editor.command and click Open Anyway.
  3. Confirm, and enter your Mac password if asked.

Or skip the file: open Terminal (in Applications, Utilities), paste this line and press Return:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/gpingelbiz/Ai/main/reel-editor/install.sh | bash

If a window asks to install Apple's command line tools, click Install, wait for it to finish, then run the installer again. If Homebrew is missing, the installer adds it and asks for your Mac password once.

2. Make a reel

  1. Drop a clip into one of the three Raw folders in Desktop/Reels. The folder decides what you get.
  2. Wait for the notification. A 1-minute clip takes about 3 minutes.
  3. Open the new folder in Desktop/Reels, named by date and number.
Drop intoYou get
Raw - Reel2026-10-21 Reel 1: the edited reel in the classic look (bold outlined captions), named after its hook
Raw - Reel with B-roll2026-10-21 Reel 2 with B-roll: the full look: graphic cards that show what you say, captions that light up word by word with a hand-drawn marker on the key word, big climax words that stamp in as you say them, and generated pictures cut in for a couple of seconds each, plus those pictures. Cards need Claude Code; pictures need Codex (below).
Raw - Long Form2026-10-22 Clips 1: a podcast, a live or a training cut into up to 5 reels, each with the full look. Wide footage follows whoever is talking and splits the screen when two people trade lines. Each clip comes with its cover, caption and the words to check; clips.txt lists the moments.
Raw - Carousel2026-10-21 Carousel 1: Instagram carousel slides made from the video, plus the slide text. Needs Codex (below).

To get two of them from one clip, drop it into both folders. For background music, put licensed tracks (mp3, m4a or wav, such as Epidemic Sound downloads) in Desktop/Reels/Music: the editor picks one that fits what you say (name files Mood - Title - Artist.mp3), low under your voice, and music.txt names it. Every folder also gets:

FileWhat it is
check these words.txtOnly when the two transcription engines disagreed on a word, with its time in the reel. Check those spots before posting.
caption.txtA caption, 3 hashtags and 3 hook ideas based on what is working for that topic right now. Needs Claude Code and the Sandcastles connector; otherwise it is skipped.
Cover 1-3.pngThree cover images to pick from when you post: your best frame with a short bold title (outlined, on a dark bar, or with one word boxed), placed so the profile grid does not crop it.
The hookIf you did not write one in the file name, the editor writes three (strongest first, from what wins on Sandcastles) and puts the first on the finished reel; all three are in caption.txt. To use another, drop the clip again named clip__Your hook here.mov.

The filename is the brief:

Name your clipResult
clip.movYour opening sentence becomes the hook
clip__Stop explaining the price.movEverything after the two underscores is the hook
clip__Stop *explaining* the price.movAsterisks pick the highlighted word
clip #nozoom.movNo punch-in zooms
clip #landscape.mov16:9 for YouTube
clip #low.mov / clip #top.movForce the hook low or high (it normally places itself around your face)
clip #music:calm.mov / clip #nomusic.movUse the Music track whose name has "calm", or no music

B-roll and carousels use Codex: say yes when the installer offers it, and sign in with your ChatGPT account. Without it, Raw - Reel still works. Codex has usage limits; when it hits one, b-roll reels come without b-roll for 20 minutes and a carousel leaves a note to drop it again.

3. Make it yours

  • Your words: open ~/.reel/dictionary.json and add your program and framework names under terms. They get highlighted and spelled right.
  • Your caption voice: edit ~/.reel/voice.md with who you talk to, words you use, and words you never use.
  • A call-to-action: add export REEL_WATCH_CTA="Comment YOURWORD" inside the reel-editor block in ~/.zshrc.
  • Change your look: double-click the installer again and pick another option.

Fix a problem

What you seeFix
Nothing happens after a dropMake sure you dropped into one of the Raw folders, not the Reels folder. A "Reel failed" notification means: rename the clip (any new name) to try again.
A word in the captions is wrongCheck check these words.txt. To fix it for good, add the word under corrections in ~/.reel/dictionary.json.
The hook covers your faceAdd #top or #low to the clip's name and drop it again.
Anything else, or to updateDouble-click the installer again. It updates and repairs, and keeps your settings.

Remove it

In Terminal, run ~/Ai/reel-editor/scripts/install-watcher.sh --uninstall to turn off the drop folder. Then delete the folders ~/Ai, ~/.reel and ~/.reel-venv, and the lines between # >>> reel-editor >>> and # <<< reel-editor <<< in ~/.zshrc. Your reels in Desktop/Reels stay.
