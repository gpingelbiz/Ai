# JORDAN EXAMPLE: BRAND & MESSAGING GUIDE

FICTIONAL sample client for testing the Content Studio. Version 1, October 2026.

## 1. Positioning

- **The one-liner:** *"I help independent gym owners keep the members they already have, so the gym stops depending on new sign-ups."*
- What Jordan is: an owner who still coaches on the floor. What Jordan is not: a marketing agency, a franchise consultant.

## 2. The Buyer

- Owners of one-location strength or functional gyms, 80 to 300 members, coaching 20+ hours a week themselves.
- Their 2am dialogue: none collected yet (missing).
- Tried: discounts, challenges, paid ads for new members.

## 4. Voice & Messaging

- Formality low, humor light, energy calm and steady.
- Beliefs: the members you have are the business. Against: chasing sign-ups to cover cancellations.
- Word bank, on-brand: members, renewals, the floor, the schedule, your coaches. Off-brand, never: hustle, grind, crush it, game changer, unlock, level up.

## 5. Visual Identity

- Navy #14202B, cream #F4EFE6, one burnt orange accent #E8704A.
- Headlines in DM Serif Display, sentence case. Body in Inter.
- AI policy: AI may draw everyday scenes of a gym or an office; never a person presented as Jordan or a member.

## 6. Content System

### Pillars (new mix)

| Pillar | Share | Job | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Retention | 40% | Teach | One habit that keeps members renewing |
| Owner life | 30% | Story | What running a small gym is really like, from Jordan's own week |
| Systems | 30% | Teach | A simple process for the front desk, the schedule or the coaches |

- Formats: carousels at 10 AM, one static at 6 PM.
- Caption rules: first line repeats the hook; 3 hashtags from #gymowner #fitnessbusiness #gymlife.
- CTA playbook: no ask on Story posts; a save cue is fine on Teach posts. No DM keyword yet.

## 8. Proof & Compliance Rules

- No member results, numbers or testimonials until Jordan supplies them. No health or body-change claims.

## 10. The Never-Do List

1. Never invent a number, member, result or quote.
2. Never promise income or member growth.
3. Never shame a member or another gym.
4. Never use hustle, grind, crush it, game changer, unlock or level up.
5. No health, weight-loss or body-change claims.
