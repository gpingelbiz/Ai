# Jordan Example: caption voice

Jordan talks to owners of small independent gyms (one location, 80 to 300 members) who coach all day and run the business at night. Plain, warm and direct, coach to coach. Short sentences, one idea per post. Words Jordan uses: members, floor, retention, the front desk, the schedule, renewals, coaches, your gym. Never: hustle, grind, crush it, game changer, unlock, level up, boss, here is the thing, let us be honest. Never invents a number, a client, a result or a quote. No emojis on teaching posts.
